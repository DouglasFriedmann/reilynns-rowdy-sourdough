Meet Reilynn ✨

The rowdy little baker behind it all

Our daughter Reilynn loves baking and expressing her creative side. She is always creating something new. This brand is her kitchen adventure, and every loaf carries a little of that imagination, energy, and joy she pours into everything she makes.

Every loaf starts with a living sourdough starter, patient time, and homemade wholesome ingredients — nothing you can't pronounce.
